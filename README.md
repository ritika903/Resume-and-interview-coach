AI-Powered Resume & Interview Coach is a smart career assistance platform designed to help students and job seekers prepare for their dream jobs. The platform analyzes a user's resume, identifies areas for improvement, and provides personalized suggestions to make the resume more professional and ATS-friendly.

It also offers AI-powered mock interviews, where users can practice answering interview questions based on their job role and receive instant feedback on their answers, communication, confidence, and areas of improvement.
